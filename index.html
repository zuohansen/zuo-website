Run by a landlord who builds AI systems

Zuo Property Management is run by me, Hansen Zuo. I own a rental home in the Atlanta area and manage it from New York. I've spent my career building software that makes fast, careful decisions at scale.

I'm a landlord too

I've used a property manager for my rental since 2021. They kept problems away from me, and I valued that.

Repairs were the problem. Quotes were passed along with little checking. Three vendors got three-quarters of my repair spending. When a tenant moved out, the house sat empty for 40 days. Once every fee was added up, I paid almost 11% of the rent. The agreement said 8%.

I started Zuo to run repairs the way I want them run for my own home: every quote checked, every dollar shown, and no markup on any bill.

Questions

What counts as a saving?

Only finished repairs. The saving is the benchmark price minus the price you paid. A skipped or delayed repair never counts.

Which cost guide do you use?

Angi, formerly Angie's List. Its cost guides are free, so you can check any price yourself. Every repair statement names the Angi page and shows the range.

Who handles leasing?

Licensed local realtors find tenants and bill you directly. A tenant can also use their own agent.
