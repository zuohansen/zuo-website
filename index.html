One base fee, and a share of what we save you

You pay a base fee of 6% of the monthly base rent. When we get a repair done for less than its benchmark price, we split the saving with you 50/50. There are no other fees.

6%

Base fee. 6% of the monthly base rent. It covers tenant messages at any hour, repairs and vendors, rent collection and your monthly statement. A typical manager charges 10% to 12% for a single-family home, according to HomeGuide.

$

Base fee: $120 a month, $1,440 a year

50%

Repair savings. For each repair, we look up the published benchmark price for that job in your area. It's published as a range, and we use the middle of it. If the price comes in below that, we split the difference with you 50/50. If it doesn't, we earn nothing on that repair.

75%

Price guarantee. No repair will cost you more than the point 75% of the way up the published benchmark price for that job in your area.

What we never charge

A real example

One owner's manager charged 8% of the rent on paper. With leasing, renewal and admin fees, the total came to 10.7% of the rent collected over almost five years. That owner is Hansen, our founder.

What counts as a saving?

Only finished repairs. The saving is the benchmark price minus the price you paid. A skipped or delayed repair never counts.

Which cost guide do you use?

The published benchmark price for that job in your area, from free public cost guides, so you can check any price yourself. Every repair statement names the guide and shows the range.

Who handles leasing?

We partner with licensed local realtors, who find tenants and bill you directly. A landlord can also use their own agent.
